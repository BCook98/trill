Setup and common questions

What is Trill?

Trill is a native remote control for Herdr on your own Mac. Sessions, shells, and coding agents run on the Mac; your iPhone lets you manage sessions, send terminal input, and view project files and previews.

What do I need to use Trill?

You need a Mac running Herdr and the Trill companion, plus an iPhone or iPad with iOS/iPadOS 18 or later. Both devices must be able to connect over a local network or a VPN such as Tailscale. Trill is still in development. Email me to ask about app access. The companion setup guide covers installation on your Mac (GitHub access required).

How does Trill connect to Herdr?

The Mac companion connects to your existing Herdr servers. Terminal input, output, scrolling and resize use an authenticated HTTPS stream. Select a live session to open it. You can rename terminals and create tabs, spaces and Git worktrees. See Herdr setup and supported actions.

How do I pair my computer?

Run the companion setup on your Mac, then choose Pair a computer → Scan pairing code in Trill. Review the computer before tapping Pair. You can paste a pairing link instead. Codes expire after ten minutes and can be used only once.

To create another code after setup, run:

trill pair
Why can't I open a terminal?

Setup starts with read-only access. Enable terminal and session control on your Mac:

trill control --enable

Enable control only for devices you trust. The session also needs a running terminal that Trill can connect to. Check Sessions → More → Connection details for connection status.

What if pairing or HTTPS fails?

Keep both devices on the same Wi-Fi or connected to the same configured VPN. Use the address generated by setup: a Bonjour .local hostname for LAN, or the full trusted Tailscale .ts.net hostname. A self-signed numeric-IP address may be rejected by iOS before certificate pinning. Create a fresh pairing code if the certificate or address changed.

Do not disable system security or send your pairing token to support.

Does disconnecting stop my work?

Disconnect closes Trill's connection and leaves the Herdr terminal running on your Mac. Manage terminal closure and history in Herdr. When you return after iOS suspends Trill, the app tries to reconnect. If iOS has terminated the app, open the terminal again yourself.

Where do my photos and prompts go?

After permission, choosing a photo immediately uploads a copy to companion storage on the connected computer. On your own Mac, that storage is private to its user account. Closing the draft does not delete the photo. A coding agent may send goals, input, and accessible files to its AI provider; check that provider's privacy settings before sharing.

The optional developer-hosted demo is shared: the developer and other paired testers can access saved session content, files, and photos. Use sample text and photos only. Sessions are scripted, with no shell or AI provider. Read the privacy policy for retention and deletion details.

How do I revoke access or delete data?

Forget computer removes the primary pairing credential from this phone; it does not revoke the device registration or delete remote data. Discard any unfinished pairing separately on the pairing screen. On your own Mac, list and revoke registered devices:

trill devices
trill revoke DEVICE_ID

On your own Mac, delete uploaded images from ~/.local/share/trill/attachments or your configured attachment directory. Remove session history through Herdr, or your terminal programs. Data already shared with an AI provider is subject to that provider's deletion controls. Uninstalling Trill does not stop remote sessions or delete their data.

For the optional shared hosted demo, email trill@bcook.io to request server-data deletion or device revocation. Leave out credentials and private content. See the privacy policy for demo retention.

Which files can I preview?

Open Files from a session to browse code and images or preview a local HTML page. The browser pane can also open a local development server. MP4, MOV, and M4V video previews download before playing and support files up to 100 MiB, subject to iOS codec support. Closing the video preview removes its temporary download from your device.

How do I report a bug?

Email the app and OS versions, what you expected, what happened, and steps to reproduce it. A screenshot can help if you remove personal information first. If you share diagnostics, check them for secrets first and include only what is relevant.
