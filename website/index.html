Your real sessions
Connect to your actual tmux session or a direct terminal over mosh. Disconnect from your phone and leave the session running on your Mac.
Write naturally
Type or dictate with the native keyboard. Add a photo from your phone. Review your draft, insert it, then press Return when you’re ready.
Xirp and Herdr, together
Use either or both on the same Mac. Xirp manages coding-agent sessions and worktrees; Herdr manages spaces, worktrees, and terminal sessions. Available actions depend on the provider.
More than one computer
Save your paired Macs and switch between them, one active computer at a time. The work on your other computers keeps running.
